### Hogwarts Grounds
- Entrance: Hogwarts's main doors, from the entrance hall.
- Unlock: always open, in every setting.
### Hogwarts Dungeons
- Entrance: from the entrance hall, down the staircase.
- Unlock: always open, in every setting.
### Hippogriff Challenge
- Entrance: Hogwarts Grounds, the path past the Care of Magical Creatures arch.
- Unlock: Hippogriff Challenge Unlock (hippogriff model)
### Doxy Challenge

- Unlock: Doxy Challenge Unlock (doxy model)
### Monster Book of Monsters Challenge
- Entrance: Hogwarts, 3rd floor, the door adjacent to the Charms classroom.
- Unlock: Monster Challenge Unlock (Monsters Book model)
### Carpe Retractum Spell Challenge
- Entrance: Hogwarts ground floor, side chamber past the shortcut portraits.
- Unlock: Carpe Retractum Challenge Unlock
  - split_challenge_spells off: the unlock also gives you Carpe Retractum.
  - split_challenge_spells on: the unlock opens the door, and Carpe Retractum is a separate item. The door doesn’t need the spell, but the checks inside do.
### Draconifors/Lapifors Spell Challenge
- Entrance: Hogwarts ground floor, side chamber past the shortcut portraits.
- Unlock: Draconifors Challenge Unlock
  - split_challenge_spells off: the unlock also gives you Draconifors and Lapifors.
  - split_challenge_spells on: the unlock opens the door, and Draconifors and Lapifors are separate items, needed for the checks inside.
